RED ROGUE MUSIC TESTER
======================

Make a MIDI in your DAW, drag it onto "DROP YOUR SONG HERE", and the game
opens playing your song on the title screen. No install needed.


QUICK START
-----------
1. Before extracting the zip: right-click it, Properties, tick "Unblock", OK.
   If Windows says it protected your PC, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Open Template.mid in your DAW. It is a small working song. Keep the four
   tracks and their names (Ch1, Ch2, Ch3, Ch4) and write your song in them.
3. Export as a MIDI file (Type 1 / multi-track, not Type 0).
4. Drag the .mid onto "DROP YOUR SONG HERE.bat". A black window shows what
   happens, then the game opens. The song starts after the intro. Press Start
   to skip the intro.
5. When a song is finished, send us the .mid file. Nothing from _engine.
   We convert it with the same tool you used, so it sounds the same in the game.


THE FOUR TRACKS (it is a Game Boy, so there are only four voices)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Ch1  Square wave. Melody or lead.
Ch2  Square wave. Harmony or second lead.
Ch3  Soft wave. Usually bass.
Ch4  Noise. Drums only.

Every channel sounds at the pitch written in your DAW.


THE LIMITS
----------
1. ONE NOTE AT A TIME PER TRACK. No chords on a single track. Spread chord
   notes over Ch1, Ch2 and Ch3 instead. The tester tells you the track, bar
   and beat of any chord it finds.

2. NOTE RANGE
     Ch1 and Ch2:  MIDI 36 to 127  (C2 and up)
     Ch3:          MIDI 24 to 119  (C1 to B8)
   Anything lower or higher is rejected with a message, because the converter
   would otherwise turn it into broken noise.

3. VOLUME
     Ch1 and Ch2: velocity sets the volume (it has 8 steps, not 127).
     Ch3: no volume control. It is always full volume, so velocity does
          nothing there. Put quiet parts on Ch1 or Ch2 instead.

4. CH3 SOUND: there are 5 wave shapes (numbered 0 to 4). The template's bass
   uses the default. If you want a different shape, tell us and we will set it.

5. DRUMS (Ch4) ONLY USE MIDI NOTES 0 TO 18. This is NOT the General MIDI drum
   map. Kick on 36 and snare on 38 do not work: every note above 18 is
   rejected. Use this table:

     MIDI  Sound          MIDI  Sound
      0    snare 1         10   snare 9
      1    snare 2         11   cymbal 1
      2    snare 3         12   cymbal 2
      3    snare 4         13   cymbal 3
      4    snare 5         14   muted snare 1
      5    triangle 1      15   triangle 3
      6    triangle 2      16   muted snare 2
      7    snare 6         17   muted snare 3
      8    snare 7         18   muted snare 4
      9    snare 8

   These are very low notes. Some DAWs call MIDI 0 "C-1" and others "C-2",
   so count up from the bottom of the keyboard rather than trusting the name.
   You may have to scroll your piano roll all the way down. The DAW Samples
   folder has all 19 drums mapped to exactly these keys, so you can hear them
   while you write.

6. LOOPING. The template has text markers named "begin" and "end" on every
   track. Move them to your loop points. Never put one in the middle of a
   note. If you do, you get an error mentioning notes that "overlap other
   events".

7. SIZE. After each run, the window prints "Song size: X of Y bytes". Stay
   under Y. A song over the limit may still play in the tester, but it cannot
   go into the real game. Repeated phrases are stored once, so reusing
   identical sections saves space. Very long songs fail with "too long".

8. YOU HAVE ONE SONG SLOT. The tester replaces the title-screen music each
   run. It does not keep or mix songs.


HEARING IT WHILE YOU WRITE (optional)
-------------------------------------
DAW Samples folder:
  Ch1 Ch2 - Square   4 square waves (12.5%, 25%, 50%, 75% pulse)
  Ch3 - Wave         the 5 wave shapes
  Ch4 - Drums        the 19 drums, on keys 0 to 18
Each folder has .sfz files (load them in the free sfizz plugin, or any
SFZ-compatible sampler) and the .wav files themselves, which loop, so any
sampler can use them. These are a rough preview. The tester is the real test.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Read the black window first; it says what to fix in plain words. If it does
not make sense, send us:
  - your .mid
  - _engine\output\last-error.txt
