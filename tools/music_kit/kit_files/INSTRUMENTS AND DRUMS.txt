INSTRUMENTS AND DRUMS (quick reference)
=======================================
The game's sound set. The DAW Samples folder previews these; the game itself
is the real test.

CH1 and CH2: square waves. Pick the pulse width in the track name.
    Square 12.5   thin, nasal      Duty 0
    Square 25     hollow           Duty 1
    Square 50     full, flute-like Duty 2
    Square 75     sounds almost the same as 25   Duty 3
  Volume follows note velocity in 8 steps.

CH3: wave channel, 15 wave shapes (put "Wave N" in the track name).
  Always full volume. Waves 0-4 are the classic ones.
     0 smooth, round        1 smooth, a little flatter
     2 hollow, flute-like   3 reedy, wobbly
     4 bright, buzzy        5 stepped, nasal
     6 organ-like           7 gritty, jagged
     8 rough, brassy        9 thin and quiet
    10 mellow, wide hump   11 bright sawtooth
    12 soft, low           13 pure, sine-like
    14 square (hollow)
  (Descriptions are first impressions from the wave shapes. Tell us if one is wrong.)

CH4: drums. 19 sounds on MIDI notes 0-18, or use General MIDI drums and let
the tester map them.
    0 snare 1      5 triangle 1    10 snare 9        15 triangle 3
    1 snare 2      6 triangle 2    11 cymbal 1       16 muted snare 2
    2 snare 3      7 snare 6       12 cymbal 2       17 muted snare 3
    3 snare 4      8 snare 7       13 cymbal 3       18 muted snare 4
    4 snare 5      9 snare 8       14 muted snare 1
One drum at a time; a hit from the first twelve and a hit from the last
seven can follow each other freely (the tester switches the drum bank for you).

NOTE RANGES
    Ch1, Ch2: MIDI 36-127    Ch3: MIDI 24-119    Ch4: MIDI 0-18 (or GM drums)

WHAT IS A GAME CHOICE, NOT A HARDWARE LIMIT
  Four voices and one note at a time per voice are Game Boy hardware. The
  19-drum set, the 15 waves and the converter's note ranges are choices made
  for this game; tell us if you need something outside them.
