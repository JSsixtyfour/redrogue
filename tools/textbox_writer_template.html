Textbox Writer

Write plain prose. It comes out as text / line / cont / para macros that fit the 18-tile dialogue box, counted the way rgbasm's charmap counts them, and drawn in the game's own font.

A blank line starts a new para. A single line break forces a new line. {wStringBuffer:12} inserts text_ram wStringBuffer and reserves 12 tiles. <PLAYER>, <RIVAL> and #MON are counted at their longest.
